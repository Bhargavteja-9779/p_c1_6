# Highlights
- A training-free nearest-neighbour detector for benign-only NetFlow intrusion detection.
- Closed-form source-host context replaces learned graph aggregation.
- Higher PR-AUC than recent graph models on both NetFlow-v2 benchmarks, on a CPU.
- No attack labels for training, model selection or thresholds.
- Shows how dedicated attacker machines inflate host-context gains.
