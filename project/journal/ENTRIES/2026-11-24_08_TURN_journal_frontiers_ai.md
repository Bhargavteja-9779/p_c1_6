# 2026-11-24 — TURN — new target journal: Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence

The user allowed Frontiers journals. Frontiers does not put Received/Accepted dates into Crossref, so I read them
from the article pages. On a first random sample of 60 Frontiers in AI articles the median was 71.5 days, which would
have passed the 72-day rule. I did not trust a pass that close to the line and enlarged the same fixed-seed sample to 150:
the median became 81 days. So it fails the rule, like every other journal with a JIF that I could check. I still switch to
it: it is a bit faster than JKSU-CIS (83.5 d), has a higher JIF (6.7 vs 6.4) and publishes many NIDS papers.
