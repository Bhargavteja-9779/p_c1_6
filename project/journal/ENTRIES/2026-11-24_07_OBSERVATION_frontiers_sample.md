# OBSERVATION — 2026-11-24 (session 2) — a small sample nearly fooled me
Frontiers deposits no review dates in Crossref, so I scraped Received/Accepted from article pages. With 80 randomly sampled Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence articles the median was 71.5 days — just under the 72-day rule, and the journal's page shows JIF 6.7 (> JKSU-CIS 6.4). Because the margin was half a day I re-sampled 300 articles (same seed, larger n): median 83.5 days, IQR 57–122. The first number was sampling noise. Frontiers CS (92 d), Big Data (93 d) and Neuroinformatics (86 d) also fail. Lesson: a median near a hard threshold needs a sample large enough that its uncertainty is smaller than the margin. Both numbers are kept in journal_selection/.
