# HYPOTHESIS H2 — 2026-11-24 — written BEFORE any test
Tree ensembles rank some target attack families below benign because a few split features act as network-specific shortcuts; the families are nevertheless close to source attacks in the full feature space.
Predictions that could prove me wrong (validation only, seeds 0–2):
P1. A source-kNN detector (score = distance-weighted share of attack neighbours among pooled source training flows) has HOIC AUROC > 0.5 and higher CIC AUROC than XGBoost. If it also inverts HOIC, H2 is wrong.
P2. LightGBM per-feature contributions (pred_contrib) on CIC HOIC flows are dominated (≥ 50 % of the negative logit mass) by ≤ 3 features. If the push towards "benign" is spread over many features, the shortcut story is wrong.
P3. Replacing only those ≤ 3 features in HOIC flows with the median value of source-attack flows ("transplant") raises HOIC recall at the source threshold above 0.5. If not, those features are not the cause.
Note on novelty: Gazi et al. (code) decompose seen vs novel families and use target-window kNN (KNN-win, a one-class target detector); a source-labelled kNN and per-family shortcut attribution/transplant were not seen in their code. Not yet checked against the broader literature.
