# RESULT — 2026-11-25 — H4 (self-shielding) supported on DEV (results_t2/diag_h4.csv, scripts/t2_diag_h4.py)
P1: shield rate (test anomaly's 1-NN in train is an anomaly) > 2× the training anomaly rate in 21/23 DEV datasets (e.g. optdigits 0.985 vs 0.029; donors 1.000 vs 0.059; cardio 0.874 vs 0.096).
P2: Spearman(kNN drop, shield rate) = 0.558 (p = 0.0056) over 23 datasets.
P3: oracle removal of training anomalies (diagnostic only) recovers 0.1252 of the mean 0.1331 AUROC drop (94 %).
Exception: smtp (train anomaly rate ≈ 0 after subsampling; its drop comes from the split, not shielding).
Caveat: this is the classic MASKING effect (clustered anomalies mask each other; cf. iForest paper's masking/swamping) — the phenomenon is not new; what is new must be the quantification on ADBench and a label-free CPU remedy that reaches clean-regime accuracy.
