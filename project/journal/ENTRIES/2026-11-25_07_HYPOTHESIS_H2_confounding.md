# HYPOTHESIS H2 — 2026-11-25 — written before testing
Looking at the per-source attack rates (UNSW 5.4 %, BoT-IoT 99.7 %, ToN-IoT 39 %, CIC-IDS2018 13 %; data/proc/prep_report.json) I realised that pooling sources creates an environment–label confound: in the pooled training set, "this flow comes from the BoT-IoT network" is itself a near-perfect attack predictor. Since the networks are trivially separable from NetFlow features (Rayamajhi 2026 reports pairwise AUC ≥ 0.996), a learner can take the shortcut "looks like the BoT network ⇒ attack", and on an unseen network it then flags whatever benign traffic happens to resemble BoT's environment.
Prediction: (i) the E7 decomposition will show benign-side failure (AUC_benign_shift well below AUC_src); (ii) reweighting so that every (network, class) cell has equal mass ("cb", alpha=1; "cb05", alpha=0.5) will raise cross-network macro-F1 and AUROC versus unweighted LightGBM, and will reduce how well the model's score predicts the source network among benign flows. If (ii) fails while (i) holds, the confound is not the mechanism.
