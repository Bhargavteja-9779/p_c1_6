# TURN — 2026-11-24 — I am leaving cross-network NetFlow NIDS
After three cycles: the original idea was already done (Gazi et al. code: BRN, feature-group ablation, failure decomposition), my remedy failed (ESF ±0, PEN −0.18), the mechanism I found (3-feature flips of CIC HOIC/Bot) holds on one target only, and a cheap ensemble built on it failed (−0.056). The pivot rule (6.5) is met: ≥ 2 cycles, novelty 9/25 (≤ 14) and the core idea already in prior work, and no mechanism that generalises across networks.
What I keep: the infrastructure (queue, resumable runner, budget ledger, report and audit scripts, autosave), the lesson that a benign control is essential for counterfactual "transplant" tests, the lesson that a median near a hard threshold needs a large sample, and the lesson that in this cloud environment the compute run dies with an idle session.
What may still be worth something later: a short negative-results note ("benign-environment normalisation and environment-feature removal do not fix cross-network NetFlow detection; the failure concentrates in a few inverted attack families") — but it overlaps heavily with Gazi et al. and Rayamajhi 2026.
