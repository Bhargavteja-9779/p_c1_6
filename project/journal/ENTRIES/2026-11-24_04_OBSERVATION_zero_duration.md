# OBSERVATION — 2026-11-24 — the exporters differ, not only the traffic
My first ESF run crashed on NaN. Tracing it: SRC_TO_DST_SECOND_BYTES is NaN or inf exactly for flows with FLOW_DURATION_MILLISECONDS = 0. In the random training pools, 15,562 UNSW flows and 18,472 BoT-IoT flows have zero duration; ToN-IoT and CIC-IDS2018 have none at all. So the four "same-schema" datasets were not produced by identically configured exporters. A model can tell the networks apart from such artefacts alone — a first concrete example of what I called an environment signature. I set the undefined rates to 0 (D12).
