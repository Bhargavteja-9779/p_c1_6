# HYPOTHESIS H5 — 2026-11-25 — label-free shield removal (written BEFORE any method run)
Leave-one-out kNN inside the training set is itself shielded (anomaly clusters protect each other), so the trust signal must be masking-resistant. Small random subsamples break anomaly clusters apart (a cluster of m anomalies in n points contributes ≈ m·ψ/n points to a subsample of size ψ).
Method family "SCREEN-kNN" (working name): (1) score every TRAINING point with a masking-resistant subsample ensemble; (2) drop the points flagged as likely anomalies; (3) kNN against the cleaned reference set.
Pre-registered variants (all counted in variants_tried.csv; DEV only, seeds 0–2, both regimes):
 A1 trust = iForest score (ψ=256), drop fixed top 10 %;   A2 same, top 20 %
 A3 trust = subsample-kNN ensemble (50 subsamples of ψ=256 points, k=1 distance of each train point to the subsample, mean), drop top 10 %;  A4 same, top 20 %
 A5 = A3 trust with an adaptive cut: drop points whose trust score exceeds Q3 + 1.5·IQR of the training scores (no contamination rate needed)
Baselines added for fairness: kNN, iForest, LOF, and subsampled kNN ensemble without cleaning (Aggarwal & Sathe 2015 style; B1: mean over 50 subsamples ψ=256 of 5-NN distance).
Success on DEV: contaminated mean AUROC ≥ kNN_clean − 3 points (≥ 83) AND clean mean AUROC ≥ kNN − 0.5 (≥ 85.6). Selection among A1–A5 by DEV mean of both regimes only; then frozen and run once on HOLDOUT + confirm seeds.
