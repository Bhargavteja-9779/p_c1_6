DIAGNOSIS (scripts/t2_diag_h6.py → results_t2/diag_h6.csv; training labels used only diagnostically):
- The mean gain is one dataset: optdigits +0.543 (kNN 0.433 → 0.976). Without optdigits the mean Δ is −0.015; H6a wins on 11/23 datasets.
- R1 (bridging) is real: on average 27 % of training anomalies end up INSIDE the core (census 86 %, landsat 64 %, Cardiotocography 56 %).
  Across datasets, Spearman(Δtest, share of anomalies in core) = −0.47: where anomalies are bridged into the majority component,
  H6 loses (fault −0.144, cardio −0.128, landsat −0.118, letter −0.112).
- R2 (multimodal normals) is structural, not just a risk: Kruskal stops as soon as one component reaches 50 %, so ~40 % of training
  normals lie OUTSIDE the core and receive positive path costs. The path cost ranks training anomalies only moderately (AUROC 0.73),
  the same level as the H5 trust scores (0.72–0.74).
So H6 works only when anomalies form a compact cluster that is far from every normal mode (optdigits, donors +0.124). Its single
"core" assumption is wrong for most ADBench data: normal data is multi-modal and anomalies are often connected to it by chains.
